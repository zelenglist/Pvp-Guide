# ⚔️ __Sprinthits__ ⚔️ _Technique Creator_ - LoCr _Name Creator_ - LoCr ## 📝 Stun Technique > Risk ⭐<:star1:1360585436468346970><:star1:1360585436468346970> > Reward ⭐⭐⭐ ## 🔻 __How to Perform__ 🔻 🗡️ **__Sprinthits deal more knockback and are meant to stun the target__**: - If you hold sprint, only the first hit of the combo will be a sprint hit and all the rest will be sweep hits. You need to keep resetting your sprint to be able to keep getting sprinthits with every one of your hits. How come the target gets stunned from a sprint hit combo? When the target gets knocked back by the sprint hit, their range gets lowered, and that allows you to keep on comboing without being touched. 🔻**__There are a few ways to reset your sprint effectively__**🔻 `1.`__"W-Taps"__- Release W and then press it again after every hit to reset the sprinthit. This might be riskier to go for than the next options i'll be giving, since it is usually countered by NoKb moves. `2.`__"Pausing W-Taps"__- Pause for a bit before pressing "w" again instead of pressing it right away. This way you will abuse the full knockback, and you can get a better stun on your opponent. `3.`__"S-Taps"__- S-Taps give the best stun on the target if done correctly. Since the target loses range while getting knocked back, if you keep s-tapping to 3 block range, your opponent won't be able to touch you. _"Crouch-tap"- Completely useless, but it resets your sprinthit and you can go for a sprinthit combo with it_ - __Sprinthit Combo__ - ⁠You can get sprinthit combos on people with either strafe OR straight-line. You have to maintain 3 block spacing [or close to it] and perfect hit timing to be able to keep the combo. - __When should you use each form of sprint reset tactic__? It is situational and depends on the spacing that you need for your next hit to maintain your stun. ✅ __Best Scenarios__: **Combos** ❌ __Worst Scenarios__: VS **Jump Reset, Hit Select** 🔻 _Clips: S-Tap, W-Tap, Pausing W-Tap_🔻 (edited)

# ⚔️ __NoKb Crit__ ⚔️ _Technique Inventor - Unknown_ _Name Created by - xYuvalx_ ## 📝 NoKb Technique > Risk ⭐⭐<:star1:1360585436468346970> > Reward ⭐⭐⭐ ## 🔻 __**How to Perform**__ 🔻 __A counter to Crit Deflection__ - After you Crit someone hold W and wait for your opponent to hit you. This way your knockback will be reduced. Only after they hit you, go for your 2nd hit; with your 2nd hit, you can either go for a Punish crit, or for a Falling Sprinthit (which depends on the situation). `1.` If you choose to use a "Punish crit" after the opponent hits you, you'll be able to stay on the target and keep your combo going without letting the opponent deflect your crits. - Make sure you don't go through your opponent [for consistent aim], stop right in front of them instead. Your KB will still be reduced in the same way. - If your opponent spaces well, you won't be able to get many NoKb Crits in a row `2.` By using a "Falling Sprinthit" after the opponent sprinthits you, they will get stunned (if done from the right range), allowing you to keep the combo going. You can also "s-tap" after the "falling sprinthit" when you need some extra space from your opponent to ensure the stun. - With correct spacing, you can end up stunning somebody no matter how close to them you land. Time your "W" key correctly for that. [can let go of w for a bit to gain more spacing] ✅ `Best Scenarios:` VS **Crit Deflection** ❌ `Worst Scenarios:` VS **Double Crits** / **S-Hold Deflection** / Repositioning / Standard Crits 🔻_Clips: NoKb Crits, NoKb Crit into Sprinthit Followup, NoKb Crits into S-Hold_🔻 (edited)
