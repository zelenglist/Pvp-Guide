***Category 3:*** 🔨 `Armour Damage Types` 🔨 The following types of damage are reduced by armour and, consequently, damage the armour itself: `-` Direct (melee) attacks from mobs and players. This includes the Strength and the Weakness effects and damage-increasing enchantments) `-` Getting hit with an arrow, a shulker bullet or a thrown trident. This includes extra damage from enchantments (except shulker bullets) `-` Getting hit with a fireball from a ghast or blaze, or a fire charge `-` Laser beam of a guardian or an elder guardian `-` Damage from Thorns enchantment, or spikes of guardians and elder guardians `-` Touching a damaging block (fire, lava, magma block, cactus or sweet berry bush) `-` Touching a puffer fish `-` Explosions `-` Fireworks with at least a firework star in the recipe `-` Lightning `-` Getting hit with a falling anvil `-` Getting hit with a snowball from a player or snow golem `-` Getting hit with a egg from a player‌ The following types of damage are not reduced by armour and have no effect on the armour itself: `-` Fall damage (including ender pearls, but Protection and Feather Falling enchantments reduce it) Ongoing damage from being on fire `-` Suffocating inside a block or due to entity cramming (also includes the world border) `-` Drowning in water (partially for turtle shells) `-` Starvation `-` Colliding with a block while flying with elytra `-` Magic (Evoker fangs, status effects and instant damage from a potion of Harming or Dragon's Breath) (but Protection does reduce magical damage‌) `-` Falling into the void or `/kill` command . ***Category 4:*** 🗡️ `Armour Penetration` 🗡️ Every incoming hp dmg reduces the armour protection by `2%`, this means that stronger attacks have the ability to ignore some of the % protection of the armour 📝 **__Example__** 📝 > **Player A** has `10` armour points (that means `40%` dmg reduction, `4%` dmg reduced per points -> `4%*10=40%`), and **Player B** attacks him with a sharpness 5 diamond sword (`10` hp dmg) > Due to the armour penetration, `20%` (`10` hp incoming dmg * `2%` armour protection reduced) of the armour dmg reduction will be ignored and only `20%` ( originally `40%`) of the incoming dmg will be negated. That means from the `10` damage, __`8` damage will be dealt__ **Look the example while you see the giphy:** (edited)

By default, only diamond and netherite armor provide toughness, with each piece granting `+2` toughness for diamond and `+3` for netherite 📝 **__Example__** 📝 > A full set of diamond which gives `8` armour toughness results in the armour getting ignored by only `1%` for each incoming hp dmg (usually `2%`). With `12` dmg taken, `3` durability points will be lost. When you take `7` hp dmg, the armour will lose `1` durability point because you didn't take the `8` hp dmg needed to lose `2` points. . ***Category 9:*** 🧱 `Resistance Effect` 🧱 Every level of the resistance effect will give a dmg reduction of `20%`. With resistance 5 you will receive a reduction of `100%`, so a higher resistance level is not necessary ❓ **__IMPORTANT QUESTION__** ❓ *How do normal armour, protection enchantments, and the resistance effect work together?* `-` Basically, all 3 of them are calculated on their own. So if your armour protects you `40%`, the resistance `40%`, and your enchantments `40%`, **that does not mean you have an overall reduction of 120%.** To get the overall protection you need to multiply the incoming dmg of the specific parts, that means when `40%` is reduced `60%` dmg is taken. So you need to calculate: > `60%x60%x60%=21.6% incommig dmg which means your protection overall is 78.4%` **Don't forget to take armour penetration and armour toughness into account** . (edited)

***Category 10:*** 💡 `Armour Tips` 💡 Here you can see some tips about armour that can help you in some decisions and games: `-` The best way to be protected against a specific kind of dmg is to have 3 pieces of protection 4 and one piece with the specific protection enchantment `-` If your sword doesn’t have the knockback enchant and you want to deal vertical kb to netherite wearers so you can crystal them, you can only do it with sprint hits. Vertical kb from normal hits is reduced too much by the armour `-` Damage dealt by effects completely bypasses armour, making it a good way to deal with highly-armoured targets . (edited)
