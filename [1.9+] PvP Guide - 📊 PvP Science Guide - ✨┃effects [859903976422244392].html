🧪 ***__Effects__*** 🧪 **Author:** `iDexy` **Translator:** `Zivkovic` **Writer:** `iDexy` In this channel all effects of the game are listed and explained how they influence the player ***Category 1:*** 📖 `Basic Things` 📖 `-` An effect is a helpful or harmful condition that affects an entity. Effects can be inflicted in various ways throughout the game, including consuming potions and some food items, being in the range of beacons and conduits, and being attacked by or close to certain mobs `-` The `/effect` command allows players to inflict effects upon themselves and other entities `-` You can get them with Normal potions, splash potions lingering potions and tipped arrows `-` To use normal potions, you just drink the potion. To use splash potions, throw the potion to the ground to get the effect of the potion. To use lingering potions, throw the potion to the ground or a block to create an effect cloud with the effect of that potion ❗ **__IMPORTANT__** ❗ `-` If you have splash potions, throw the potion up in the air to get the maximum power of the potion (the effect will last longer) `-` When a creeper has an effect and explodes, it leaves an area effect cloud for each effect that it had before it died `-` Players can open their inventory to see any current effects afflicted upon them, as well as its level and duration `-` However, it is not possible to apply the same effect multiple times, even if they are of different levels (e.g. Strength and Strength 2) `-` Positive effects have blue text in potion information, and in are displayed on the upper row of effects in the heads up display, while negative effects have red potion text and are displayed in the bottom row. Neutral effects have the blue potion text and are listed with the negative effects in the bottom row in the heads up display . (edited)

***Category 2:*** 🩺 `Behaviour` 🩺 **Basic Things:** `-` Any entities inflicted by an effect are affected in various ways for an amount of time `-` During the effect, spiral-shaped particles emanate from the position of the inflicted entity `-` For most effects, higher levels increase the strength of the effect ❗ **__IMPORTANT__** ❗ `-` Note that even with commands, effects cannot be made to last forever. If an effect is set to last a time greater than `32766 (215−2) ticks, 27 minutes and 18 seconds, or 1638 seconds`, it displays as `^^.^^`, but still continues ticking down and eventually expires .
