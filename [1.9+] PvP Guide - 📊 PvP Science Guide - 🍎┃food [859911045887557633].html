***Category 2:*** 👨‍🍳 `Hunger` 👨‍🍳 Hunger is a player-specific feature of Minecraft that regulates player's certain abilities (health regeneration and the ability to sprint) the value of which is managed by the player's activity **Basic things:** `-` The player's hunger value is shown on the heads-up display in the form of a hunger bar (also called food bar), which is similar to health bar (located above the hotbar), located opposite to it and represented by ten drumsticks (🍗 ) `-` __One half of a drumstick represents one hunger point or half-unit of hunger__, cuz the full bar consists of twenty hunger points. **like we said in <#436138841493995531>.** It is replenished by eating food, and decreased by player's actions such as sprinting, digging or attacking **(We will see this in next categories)** `-` Various levels of player's hunger control health regeneration (or depletion) and the ability to sprint `-` If the hunger bar is at zero, the player's health depletes `-` The specific effects are described in **next categories** `-` The hunger value does not drain on Peaceful difficulty and regenerates if it is not at the maximum value `-` The hunger indicator is shown above the hotbar, to the other side of the health bar . ***Category 4:*** 🗳️ `Hunger and Saturation System` 🗳️ The hunger and saturation system is controlled by the variables which are listed below. `1.` **foodLevel** - The player's current hunger level, shown visually on the hunger bar. Its initial value on world creation or respawn is 20 hunger points (10 drumsticks) `2.` **foodSaturationLevel** - The player's current saturation level, which determines how fast the hunger level depletes and is controlled by the kinds of food the player eats. Its maximum value always equals foodLevel's value and decreases with the hunger level. Its initial value on world creation or respawn is 5. Its maximum is 20. `3.` **foodExhaustionLevel** - The player's current exhaustion level, which determines how fast the saturation and hunger points decrease. This value is increased by the player's actions. The initial value is zero. When it reaches the maximum value of 4, it resets to zero and one point is subtracted from the foodSaturationLevel, or when saturation is gone then the foodLevel. The units of foodLevel are hunger points, the units of foodSaturationLevel are saturation points, and the foodExhaustionLevel is the system to control the decrease of hunger and Saturation points. The foodExhaustionLevel is controlled by actions you do ingame. You can imagine the system as a clock where saturation points are minutes and food Exhaustion is seconds. When enough Exhaustion points are gone one saturation point will be removed. If the Saturation is gone, food Exhaustion will remove hunger points. And when you reach zero hunger Points the time runs out and you lose health Points due to starvation damage, until you fill it again with food . (edited)

📝 **__Example__** 📝 > If a golden carrot (giving `6 hunger points (3 drumsticks) and 14.4 saturation points`) is eaten by a player whose hunger bar is at `9 (4.5 drumsticks)` and saturation is below `1`, its value increases to `15`, and the golden carrot's saturation potential is fully used. However, if the hunger level is `8`, its value increases to `14` and only `14` saturation points are used . (edited)

***Category 5:*** 😋 `Effects of Hunger` 😋 Various hunger levels lead to various effects on the player: `1.` When the hunger bar is at `20 (10 drumsticks)` and there is any saturation left, health regenerates `1 heart` every `1⁄2 second` `2.` When the hunger bar is at `18 (9 drumsticks)` or more, health slowly regenerates 1 heart every `4 seconds` `3.` When hunger is over `17 (8.5 drumsticks)`, the player's health regenerates. If it falls to `6 (drumsticks)`, the player loses the ability to sprint `4.` If the hunger bar is at `6 (3 drumsticks)` points or below, then the player cannot sprint `5.` When the hunger bar is at `0`, the player's health depletes at a rate of `1 heart` every `4 seconds` (this makes sleeping impossible). On **Easy difficulty**, the player's health stops dropping at `10` hearts, on **Normal**, it stops at `1` heart, and on **Hard**, it keeps draining until either the player eats something or starves to death. Resistance and armour do not reduce starvation damage Switching to Peaceful difficulty eliminates all penalties above, and rapidly restores the player's hunger bar and health . (edited)

. **How much does saturation heal the player?** With `20 saturation points` the shield is full. Take a look at the table in the saturation chapter to see how much you need to eat to get a full shield. A full shield can heal `13 hp (6.5 hearts) + 2 hp from the hunger bar (1 full heart)` so overall you get an extra `15 health points` The speed of the regeneration from saturation is `2 hp (1 heart) per second`. When you run out of saturation, you will gain an extra `2 hp (1 heart)` from the hunger points over the course of `8 seconds` 📜 ***__Special Rule__*** 📜 When you have an amount of saturation with a decimal, the saturation will get rounded up in the final calculation, meaning if you have `12.2 saturation it is 13 saturation` There are different methods to use this “saturation shield” to your advantage: `-` First of all the pretty basic one, try to always eat right before a fight so you have a charged "shield" and when it comes to hit trades, you have a better chance of winning because of your fuller shield `-` Try to avoid doing many actions that cost saturation (like sprinting) shortly before a fight begins. Because otherwise your saturation will be used for these actions and not for healing. For example, if you only move without sprinting you don't lose any saturation points, and if a fight can't be avoided anyway its better to let them attack you and save your saturation for the fight instead of running `-` if your saturation shield is almost empty and only a few saturation points are remaining, to recharge it faster you can quickly hit yourself with fire or arrows to remove the remaining points so your hunger goes down and you can reload it with some more food. You can also build a 2 block high ceiling to sprint jump under it to remove the saturation (edited)
