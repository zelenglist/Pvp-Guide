# ⚔️ __**Hit Select**__ ⚔️ ## 📝 Stun / NoKb Technique > Risk ⭐⭐<:star1:1360585436468346970> > Reward ⭐⭐⭐ ## 🔻 __**How to Perform**__ 🔻 Let someone hit you, and then sprint hit them back immediately. If done correctly, the target will get stunned. Hit Select will REDUCE your knockback by 40% of the remaining KB after you hit the target. - If you want to maximize the KB reduction, hit the target as fast as you can after you get hit. Best used at longer ranges. - If you are too close to your opponent, after they hit you, delay your Hit Select to be able to gain enough distance to stun them. Hit Selecting in a first hit scenario can be risky, because if your opponent spaces correctly you'll get stunned without hitting back. ✅ __Best Scenarios__: VS **Sprint hits** / **S-Hold Deflection**. When you're already close to the target, for example in the middle of trades. ❌ __Worst Scenarios__: Hit selecting from very close range, Hit Selecting on first hits [usually]. (edited)

# ⚔️ __**Jump Reset**__ ⚔️ ## 📝 NoKb Technique > Risk ⭐⭐<:star1:1360585436468346970> > Reward ⭐⭐<:star1:1360585436468346970> ## 🔻 __**How to Perform**__ 🔻 Jump while sprinting forward right as someone hits you. Has to be done with perfect timing by knowing your opponent's hit timing. If done correctly, your knockback will be reduced significantly. Can also be spammable and used VS any sprinthit that isnt a "stun hit". ‼️ __Jump Resets are easily countered by__ - `1`.__Delaying a hit__ intentionally to mess with the jump timing. `2`.__Stun Hits__: Against people who are good at spacing, Jump Resets will be a BAD option VS their combos in many cases. Even if you will eventually succeed breaking free from a combo, you might take too much damage at that point for it to be worth it. - If you feel like you won't be able to hold a combo after u stun someone, you can jump for a crit while you still have the advantage. __So when SHOULD you use Jump Resets__? - `1.` Learn when your opponent doesn't have enough distance to stun you with their next hit. This will be the best scenario to attempt it. `2.` Jump Resets can be good to use on first hits, if both go for a ground hit sprinthit. This will reduce the chances of getting stunned from the exchange and can potentially help you stun them instead. ✅ __Best Scenarios__: VS **Sprinthits** ❌ __Worst Scenarios__: VS **Perfect Stun Hits / Delayed Hit Timing.** 🔻_Clips: Jump Resets, Delayed hit timing VS Jump Resets, Perfect Stun Hits VS Jump Resets [clip by Kylaz]_ 🔻 (edited)
